A normal 6-hour day

  • 2 hours: trainer demo and discussion.
  • 3 hours: hands-on lab. This is where you learn.
  • 1 hour: practice, review and feedback.

Attendance, grading and certificate

  • Attendance: you need at least 80% of the 300 hours to receive the certificate.
  • Grades: 50% module deliverables, 20% quizzes, 30% capstone project.
  • Pass mark: 60% overall and a passing capstone. Your trainer will confirm the final pass mark on Day 1.
  • Portfolio: every module ends with a deliverable. Keep all of them, because they become your portfolio.
  • Missed a day? Tell your trainer the same day and ask for the lab task so you can catch up.

AI ethics rules

  1. Disclose AI use. Write which tool you used on every assignment you submit.
  2. Check every fact. AI can be confidently wrong. Verify names, numbers, dates and quotes against a real source.
  3. Protect private data. Never paste passwords, NID or bank numbers, or private client and classmate information into any tool.
  4. Respect copyright. Use only images, music and text you have the right to use. Paraphrasing does not remove the need to cite.
  5. No voice or face copying. Never clone a voice or edit someone's photo without their written permission.
  6. Do your own thinking. Use AI to help you learn and work faster, not to skip the learning. Exams are done without AI help unless the task says otherwise.
