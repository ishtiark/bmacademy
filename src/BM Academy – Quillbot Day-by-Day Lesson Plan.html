Day 16 hours

Paraphraser & Summarizer

  • 1 hAccount setup, interface tour, free vs premium limits. Rule: paraphrasing never removes the need to cite the source.
  • 1 hDemo: paraphrase modes (Standard, Fluency, Formal, Simple, Creative, Shorten, Expand), synonym slider and frozen words.
  • 3 hLab: paraphrase the same 150-word passage in 4 modes, compare the results, and fix any changed meaning by hand.
  • 1 hLab: summarizer. Make a 100-word and a 50-word summary of one article, then check facts against the original.

Sample task

Paste a 150-word passage. Run Standard, Formal, Simple and Shorten. Mark every sentence where the meaning changed.

Deliverable

4-mode comparison sheet + 2 checked summaries

Day 26 hours

Citations, Co-Writer & Research Workflow

  • 1 hDemo: citation generator in APA and MLA. Entering source details correctly and checking the output.
  • 2 hLab: create citations for 10 sources (book, website, journal article, report) and build a reference list.
  • 2 hLab: research workflow. Summarize 3 articles, write a short literature paragraph in your own words, and cite each source.
  • 1 hDiscussion: academic integrity, AI-detection limits, and how to disclose AI help.

Sample task

Source: article URL and author details entered in the citation tool. Output checked by hand against the APA format.

Deliverable

10-source reference list + cited literature paragraph

Day 33 hours

Assessment & Showcase

  • 1 hPractical exam: summarize a 600-word text, paraphrase 2 paragraphs, and add 3 correct citations.
  • 2 hShowcase of the cited summary, feedback round and grading.

Sample task

Present the paraphrasing and citation workflow, including a manual accuracy check.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% cited summary, 40% practical exam

Some Quillbot modes and limits depend on the free or premium plan, so check what is available before Day 1. Paraphrasing is not a substitute for citing, and students must always disclose AI help. Features change often, so check the interface the day before class.
