Day 16 hours

Setup, Brand Voice & Short Copy

  • 1 hAccount setup, workspace tour, templates and apps. Check what the trial or school plan includes.
  • 1 hDemo: brand voice and company knowledge. Adding a style guide, product facts and sample texts.
  • 3 hLab: create a brand voice for a mock business, then write 10 product descriptions and 10 social captions in that voice.
  • 1 hReview, quiz, and a fact-check of every product claim.

Sample prompt

Write 3 product descriptions for [PRODUCT]. Audience: [AUDIENCE]. Tone: [TONE]. Use only these facts: [FACTS]. Max 60 words each.

Deliverable

Brand voice document + 20 short copies

Day 26 hours

Ads, Blog Posts & SEO Basics

  • 1 hDemo: ad copy structure (hook, benefit, proof, call to action) for Facebook and Google ads.
  • 2 hLab: write 5 ad variations and 3 headline versions for the same offer.
  • 2 hLab: blog post workflow. Pick a keyword, write the outline, draft the post, add a title and meta description.
  • 1 hEdit the draft by hand. Remove invented facts and repeated phrases.

Sample prompt

Write a blog outline for the keyword [KEYWORD]. Include a title, 5 headings, and a meta description under 155 characters.

Deliverable

5 ads + 1 SEO blog post (600 words)

Day 36 hours

Campaign Project & A/B Testing

  • 1 hDemo: planning a campaign. Goal, audience, channels, message and calendar.
  • 3 hLab: build a one-week campaign for a real or mock product: 5 posts, 2 ads, 1 email and 1 blog post, all in one voice.
  • 2 hLab: A/B test. Run 3 headline versions on classmates or a small test audience and record which one wins and why.

Sample prompt

Create a one-week campaign for [PRODUCT]. Goal: [GOAL]. Channels: Facebook, email, blog. Give a daily plan with copy for each post.

Deliverable

Campaign pack + A/B test results

Day 42 hours

Assessment & Showcase

  • 1 hPractical exam: write one ad, one product description and one email in the brand voice within 45 minutes, then edit them.
  • 1 hCampaign presentation, feedback and grading.

Sample prompt

No new prompt. Students use their own brand voice document.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% campaign pack, 40% practical exam

Jasper is a paid tool, so confirm trial or school access for every student before Day 1. If access is limited, run the same labs in ChatGPT or Gemini. Feature names change often, so check the interface the day before class, and have students fact-check every claim in generated copy.
