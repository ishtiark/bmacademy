Invideo Module

Day-by-day lesson plan for Module 10: prompt-to-video, editing, platform formats, repurposing content and a promo video project.

Day 16 hours

Invideo AI Basics: Prompt to Video

  • 1 hAccount setup, interface tour, plan limits, watermark and credits. Rule: use only media you have the right to use.
  • 1 hDemo: video prompt formula. Topic, audience, length, tone, platform and voice.
  • 3 hLab: generate 5 short videos from prompts. Read the script first, edit it, then approve and compare versions.
  • 1 hReview, quiz, and a prompt log of what worked.

Sample prompt

Create a 45-second vertical video promoting [PRODUCT] to [AUDIENCE]. Friendly tone, clear call to action at the end, English voice-over.

Deliverable

5 generated videos + prompt log

Day 26 hours

Editing: Scenes, Captions & Branding

  • 1 hDemo: editing by chat commands and in the timeline. Replace media, trim, reorder and change voice.
  • 3 hLab: edit one video three ways: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels and 1:1 for Facebook. Fix captions and timing.
  • 1 hLab: add brand logo, colours and a closing screen.
  • 1 hPeer review and practical quiz.

Sample prompt

Edit command: shorten scene 2 to 4 seconds, replace the stock clip with a close-up of the product, and add bold captions.

Deliverable

1 video exported in 3 formats

Day 36 hours

Content Videos: Hooks & Repurposing

  • 1 hDemo: the first 3 seconds. Hook types, storyboard and shot list.
  • 3 hLab: turn a blog post into a 60-second video, and a product page into a 30-second ad.
  • 2 hLab: write 3 different hooks for one video, make all 3 versions, and compare which one holds attention best.

Sample prompt

Turn this article into a 60-second explainer. Start with a question hook, use 5 scenes, and end with a one-line summary.

Deliverable

2 repurposed videos + hook comparison

Day 44 hours

Promo Video Project

  • 1 hPlan: goal, audience, script and storyboard for a 60-second promo for a real or mock business.
  • 3 hProduce: generate, edit, add brand elements, captions and music, then export in 2 formats.

Sample prompt

Create a 60-second promo for [BUSINESS]. Include an offer, 3 benefits, and contact details in the final scene.

Deliverable

60-second promo in 2 formats

Day 53 hours

Assessment & Showcase

  • 1 hPractical exam: make a 30-second video from a written brief within 45 minutes.
  • 2 hScreening of promo videos, feedback and grading.

Sample prompt

No new prompt. Students bring their prompt log and storyboard.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% promo video, 40% practical exam

Free plans may add watermarks or limit export length and credits, so confirm access for every student before Day 1. Students should check stock media and music rights, and label AI-generated videos when they publish them. Features change often, so check the interface the day before class.
