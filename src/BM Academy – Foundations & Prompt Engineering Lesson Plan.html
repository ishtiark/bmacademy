Day 16 hours

How AI Works & Using It Safely

  • 1 hCourse orientation. Map of the 11 tools: chat, writing, design, video and voice. Which tool is for which job.
  • 1 hDemo: how a language model works in simple terms. What it does well, where it fails, and why it sometimes invents facts.
  • 2 hLab: ask one chat tool 10 questions with known answers. Mark each answer right, partly right or wrong, and note the pattern.
  • 1 hSafety rules: no passwords, NID, bank or client data. Check terms of use, copyright and AI disclosure.
  • 1 hReview and quiz.

Sample prompt

Ask: What is the capital of Australia? Then ask: Are you sure? Then ask for a source. Note how the answer changes.

Deliverable

10-question accuracy log + personal AI safety rules

Day 26 hours

Prompt Engineering Techniques

  • 1 hDemo: the RTCF formula (Role, Task, Context, Format) and the common mistakes: vague, too broad, no format.
  • 1 hDemo: techniques. Give examples, ask for step by step, set limits, ask the AI to question you first, and ask for a second version.
  • 3 hLab: rewrite 10 weak prompts for writing, study, business and design tasks. Compare results in a table.
  • 1 hPeer review: swap prompts and improve each other's.

Sample prompt

Act as a patient maths tutor. Help a high-school student understand linear equations. Explain each step, ask up to three questions if the request is unclear, and finish with one practice problem.

Deliverable

10 before/after prompts with results table

Day 36 hours

Verification, Prompt Library & Project

  • 1 hDemo: verifying answers. Ask for sources, check 2 independent sources, use a follow-up check prompt, and test with numbers.
  • 2 hLab: fact-check 5 AI answers. Mark each claim supported, unsupported or false.
  • 3 hProject: build a prompt library of 25 tested prompts, grouped by tool and task, each with a sample result and a note on what to improve.

Sample prompt

Check your answer: list every factual claim above, rate your confidence in each, and say which I should verify first.

Deliverable

Prompt library of 25 tested prompts + 5 fact-checks

Day 42 hours

Assessment & Showcase

  • 1 hPractical exam: improve 5 weak prompts and fact-check 2 AI answers within 45 minutes.
  • 1 hLibrary showcase, feedback and grading.

Sample prompt

Present the prompt library and demonstrate how verification improved one result.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% prompt library, 40% practical exam

Run the labs in any one chat tool that every student can open for free, then repeat key prompts in the other tools later in the course. Features change often, so check the interface the day before class.
