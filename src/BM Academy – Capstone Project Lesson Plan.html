Day 16 hours

Brief, Research & Copy

  • 1 hKick-off: select a real business for the capstone or use an instructor-approved practice brief. Write a one-page brief: goal, audience, budget, channels, deadline.
  • 2 hResearch with Gemini and Deepseek: market, competitors, customer needs. Verify 5 claims against sources.
  • 2 hWrite the core copy with ChatGPT and Jasper: slogan, 5 social posts, 2 ads, 1 blog post, 1 email.
  • 1 hCheck the copy with Grammarly and Quillbot. Mentor review of the brief and plan.

Sample task

Use the approved capstone brief to define a measurable goal, target audience, location, campaign period, budget and channels. Verify all claims against cited sources.

Deliverable

Signed-off brief + research notes + copy pack

Day 26 hours

Design & Video Production

  • 1 hPlan the visuals: brand colours, fonts, logo and shot list. Set up a Canva brand kit.
  • 2 hDesign in Canva and Pixlr: 5 social posts, 1 poster, cleaned product photos.
  • 2 hProduce a 60-second promo video in Invideo from the approved script.
  • 1 hAdd the voice-over with Eleven Labs and match the timing to each scene.

Sample task

Create a 60-second promo for the organization in the approved capstone brief. Open with a hook, show three verified benefits, and finish with the approved offer and contact details.

Deliverable

Design set + promo video with voice-over

Day 36 hours

Assembly, Quality Check & Pitch Prep

  • 1 hQuality checklist: facts, spelling, brand consistency, image and music rights, AI labels, file names.
  • 2 hAssemble the final pack in one shared folder. Fix problems found in the checklist.
  • 2 hBuild a 10-slide pitch deck in Canva or Copilot: problem, plan, samples, results to expect, next steps.
  • 1 hRehearse the 8-minute pitch with a classmate and write down 3 improvements.

Sample task

Pitch deck: 10 slides, max 25 words per slide. Slide 1 goal, slides 2-3 research, slides 4-8 work samples, slide 9 timeline, slide 10 next steps.

Deliverable

Final campaign pack + 10-slide pitch deck

Day 42 hours

Final Presentation & Portfolio Review

  • 1 hEach student pitches for 8 minutes, then answers 2 questions from the panel.
  • 1 hGrading, written feedback, and uploading the project to the student portfolio.

Sample task

Present the completed campaign pack and explain its research, creative decisions, validation checks and next steps.

Deliverable

Final grade: see the capstone rubric below
