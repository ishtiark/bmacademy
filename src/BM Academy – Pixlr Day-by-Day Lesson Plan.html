Day 16 hours

Editor Basics, Layers & Retouching

  • 1 hAccount setup, Pixlr editor options, opening, saving and exporting. Check the free plan limits.
  • 1 hDemo: crop, resize, rotate, layers, masks, and brightness, contrast and colour adjustments.
  • 3 hLab: fix 8 photos: tilted, dark, low contrast, wrong colours, cluttered edges. Keep an original copy of each.
  • 1 hReview, quiz, and a before and after critique.

Sample task

Task: fix a dark, tilted product photo. Crop to 1:1, correct brightness and contrast, and sharpen slightly. Export as JPG under 500 KB.

Deliverable

8 corrected photos with originals

Day 26 hours

AI Tools & Product Photo Cleanup

  • 1 hDemo: AI background removal, object removal, generative fill and expand. Check credits and limits.
  • 3 hLab: clean 10 product photos. Remove the background, add a white or colour background, remove marks and add a soft shadow.
  • 1 hLab: use generative fill or expand to extend one image for a wide banner.
  • 1 hDiscussion: honest product photos, labelling AI edits, and permission for photos of people.

Sample task

Generative fill prompt: a clean light grey tabletop with soft natural shadow, no objects, no text.

Deliverable

10 catalogue-ready product photos

Day 36 hours

Collage, Banners & Export

  • 1 hDemo: platform sizes for Facebook cover, Instagram post and story, YouTube thumbnail and web banner.
  • 3 hLab: design a promo banner from scratch, a 4-photo collage and a YouTube thumbnail.
  • 1 hLab: export for web. Compare JPG, PNG and WebP by quality and file size.
  • 1 hPeer review and practical quiz.

Sample task

Banner brief: [BUSINESS], offer: [OFFER]. Size 1200 x 628. One headline, one product image, one button-style call to action.

Deliverable

Banner + collage + thumbnail

Day 42 hours

Assessment & Showcase

  • 1 hPractical exam: clean and resize 3 supplied photos for catalogue use, and make one banner in 45 minutes.
  • 1 hPortfolio showcase of before and after images, feedback and grading.

Sample task

No new task. Students use their own saved settings and size list.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% before/after portfolio, 40% practical exam

Some Pixlr AI tools and exports depend on the free or paid plan and may use credits, so check what is available before Day 2. Pixlr has more than one editor, so confirm which one your lab uses. Features change often, so check the interface the day before class.
