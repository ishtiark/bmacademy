Day 16 hours

Canva Basics & Design Principles

  • 1 hAccount setup, interface tour, templates, elements, uploads, folders, sharing and download formats.
  • 1 hDemo: design basics. Alignment, spacing, contrast, two fonts maximum, a 3-colour palette.
  • 3 hLab: recreate 3 template designs from scratch, then redesign one bad poster using the basics.
  • 1 hReview, quiz, and a critique round.

Sample task

Task: redesign a crowded poster. Keep one headline, one image and one call to action. Use 2 fonts and 3 colours only.

Deliverable

3 recreated designs + 1 redesign

Day 26 hours

Brand Kit & Social Media Posts

  • 1 hDemo: Brand Kit with logo, colours and fonts. Check what your plan includes.
  • 3 hLab: create a brand kit for a mock business, then design 10 matching posts in 3 formats: square, story and carousel.
  • 2 hLab: schedule or export the posts, and test names and sizes for Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

Sample task

Mock business: [NAME]. Colours: [3 HEX CODES]. Fonts: [2 FONTS]. Design 10 posts: 4 offers, 3 tips, 3 announcements.

Deliverable

Brand kit + 10-post social media kit

Day 36 hours

AI Tools in Canva

  • 1 hDemo: Magic Studio tools. Text to image, Magic Edit, Magic Eraser, background remover and Magic Write. Check credits and plan limits.
  • 3 hLab: create 5 AI images, remove backgrounds from 5 product photos, and swap objects with Magic Edit.
  • 1 hLab: write copy with Magic Write, then edit it by hand.
  • 1 hDiscussion: image rights, labelling AI images, and checking outputs.

Sample task

Text to image: a clean product photo of [PRODUCT] on a white table, soft daylight, top view, no text.

Deliverable

AI image set (5) + 5 cleaned product photos

Day 46 hours

Posters, Flyers & CV Design

  • 1 hDemo: print basics. A4 size, bleed, resolution and exporting as PDF Print.
  • 3 hLab: design an event poster and a business flyer, front and back.
  • 2 hLab: design a one-page CV in two layouts, then check readability on a phone and on paper.

Sample task

Poster brief: [EVENT], [DATE], [PLACE]. Headline max 6 words. One main image. A QR code placeholder at the bottom.

Deliverable

Poster + flyer + 2 CV layouts

Day 56 hours

Presentations & Video

  • 1 hDemo: presentation structure. One idea per slide, big text, charts, and presenter notes.
  • 3 hLab: build a 12-slide deck with a title, agenda, data slide, timeline, and closing slide. Add transitions sparingly.
  • 2 hLab: make a 30-second vertical video with text animation, music and captions. Export as MP4.

Sample task

Deck topic: [TOPIC]. 12 slides. Max 25 words per slide. Add speaker notes for every slide.

Deliverable

12-slide deck + 30-second video

Day 65 hours

Brand Identity Project & Assessment

  • 3 hProject: build a brand identity kit for a mock business: logo, palette, fonts, 5 social posts, 1 poster, 1 mini deck.
  • 1 hPractical exam: design a promotional post and a flyer from a written brief in 45 minutes.
  • 1 hShowcase, feedback and grading.

Sample task

No new prompt. Students bring their brand kit and a written brief.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% brand identity kit, 40% practical exam

Some Canva features, such as Brand Kit options and the Magic Studio AI tools, depend on the plan and may use credits. Check what your education or team account includes before Day 2. Features change often, so check the interface the day before class.
