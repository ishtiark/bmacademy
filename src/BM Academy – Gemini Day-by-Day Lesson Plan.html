Gemini Module

Day-by-day lesson plan for Module 04: Gemini with Google Workspace, multimodal files, Deep Research and Gems.

30hours
5days
6hours per day
5deliverables
Day 16 hours

Getting Started & Prompting

  • 1 hAccount setup, interface tour, model choice, chat history and privacy settings. Never paste sensitive data.
  • 1 hDemo: the RTCF prompt formula in Gemini, follow-up questions, and asking for sources.
  • 3 hLab: 10 prompt rewrites. Practise summaries, comparisons, tables and tone changes in English and Bangla.
  • 1 hReview, quiz, save best prompts.

Sample prompt

Act as a study coach. Explain [TOPIC] in simple Bangla, give 3 real-life examples, then 5 quiz questions with answers.

Deliverable

Personal library of 15 tested prompts

Day 26 hours

Gemini with Google Workspace

  • 1 hDemo: Gemini with Gmail, Docs, Drive and Sheets. Check which features your Google account has enabled.
  • 3 hLab: summarize long email threads, draft replies, write a Doc from an outline, and ask Gemini to build formulas in Sheets.
  • 1 hLab: organise a Drive folder and ask questions across several documents.
  • 1 hPeer review and practical quiz.

Sample prompt

Summarize this email thread in 5 bullet points. List the decisions made and the actions I still need to take, with deadlines.

Deliverable

Workspace workflow set: 3 emails, 1 Doc, 1 Sheet

Day 36 hours

Images, Documents & Multimodal Work

  • 1 hDemo: uploading images, PDFs and screenshots, and asking precise questions about them.
  • 2 hLab: extract text and tables from a scanned page, describe and critique 5 images, and explain a chart.
  • 2 hLab: summarize a long PDF and a video transcript, then turn each into a one-page study note.
  • 1 hDiscussion: accuracy limits, checking outputs, and copyright when using other people's files.

Sample prompt

Read this PDF. Give me a one-page summary, the 5 most important points, and 3 questions I should still ask the author.

Deliverable

3 one-page study notes from different sources

Day 46 hours

Deep Research & Content Planning

  • 1 hDemo: Deep Research. Writing a clear research question and reading the plan before approving it.
  • 3 hLab: research a local market topic, then verify at least 5 claims against the cited sources. Remove anything unsupported.
  • 2 hLab: build a 30-day content calendar with post ideas, formats and posting times for a small business.

Sample prompt

Research the current market for [PRODUCT] in Bangladesh. Include key competitors, price ranges and customer needs. Cite all sources.

Deliverable

Researched brief with sources + 30-day content calendar

Day 56 hours

Gems Project & Assessment

  • 1 hDemo: Gems. Name, role, rules, tone and example answers.
  • 3 hLab: build a Gem for a real task (for example a customer support helper or a study tutor) and test it with 10 questions.
  • 2 hAssessment: practical exam of 3 tasks, then showcase of the Gem and the research brief.

Sample prompt

You are [GEM NAME]. Your goal is [GOAL]. Answer in [LANGUAGE] in a [TONE] tone. If you are unsure, say so and ask a question.

Deliverable

Final grade: 40% research brief, 30% Gem, 30% practical exam

Before Day 2: Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Drive and Sheets depends on the Google account type and settings, so test with each student's account first. Feature names such as Deep Research and Gems change often, so check the interface the day before class.
