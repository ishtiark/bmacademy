Day 16 hours

Setup, Grammar & Clarity

  • 1 hAccount setup, browser extension, web editor and desktop app. Setting goals: audience, formality, domain and tone.
  • 1 hDemo: reading suggestions, accepting or rejecting them, and learning the rule behind each fix.
  • 3 hLab: correct 5 error-filled texts (emails, essay, social post, CV, report). Log the 10 most common mistakes you make.
  • 1 hReview, quiz, personal mistake checklist.

Sample task

Original text pasted into the editor, goal set to: Audience = General, Formality = Neutral, Domain = Business. Accept only suggestions you can explain.

Deliverable

5 corrected texts + personal mistake checklist

Day 26 hours

AI Writing, Tone & Plagiarism Check

  • 1 hDemo: AI rewrite, shorten, change tone, and reply suggestions in the editor.
  • 2 hLab: rewrite the same paragraph in 3 tones (formal, friendly, persuasive) and compare the results.
  • 2 hLab: write a professional email, a cover letter and a short report, then run a clarity and tone check on each.
  • 1 hDemo and lab: plagiarism checker, citing sources, and writing in your own words. Check which features need a paid plan.

Sample task

Make this paragraph more concise and professional. Keep the meaning and keep it under 80 words.

Deliverable

3 tone versions + 3 professional documents

Day 33 hours

Assessment & Portfolio

  • 1 hPractical exam: edit one 300-word text with 25 planted errors within 30 minutes, then explain 5 of the fixes.
  • 2 hPortfolio showcase: 5 before and after documents, a feedback round, and grading.

Sample task

Review the edited portfolio against the saved style settings and proofreading checklist.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% portfolio, 40% practical exam

Grammarly mainly supports English writing, so use English texts for the labs. Some features, such as the plagiarism checker and full AI tools, may need a paid plan, so check what the free plan includes before Day 1. Features change often, so check the interface the day before class.
