How every module runs

  • Split: 30% concept demo, 60% hands-on lab, 10% review and quiz.
  • Daily pacing: the 300 instructional hours are distributed across 57 teaching days (about 5 hours 16 minutes per day on average). Daily hours vary by module; follow the day-by-day lesson plans.
  • Assessment: each module ends with a graded deliverable (listed above) kept in the student portfolio.
  • Grading: 50% module deliverables, 20% quizzes, 30% capstone.
  • Ethics rule: students always disclose AI use, fact-check outputs, and never clone a voice or image without consent.
  • Certificate: awarded at 80% attendance and a passing capstone.
