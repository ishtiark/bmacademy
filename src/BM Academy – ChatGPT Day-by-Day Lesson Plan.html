ChatGPT Module

Day-by-day lesson plan for Module 01. Every day follows demo, then lab, then review, and ends with a graded deliverable.

40hours
7days
6hours per day
7deliverables
Day 16 hours

Getting Started & Prompt Engineering

  • 1 hAccount setup, interface tour, chat history, model choice. Privacy rule: never paste passwords, NID or bank details.
  • 1 hDemo: the RTCF prompt formula (Role, Task, Context, Format) and iterating with follow-ups.
  • 3 hLab: rewrite 10 weak prompts. Ask for tables, step lists, different tones and word limits.
  • 1 hReview, quiz, save best prompts to a personal library.

Sample prompt

Act as a career counsellor in Bangladesh. I am a diploma graduate in IT. Give me a 5-step plan to get my first job in 3 months. Format: numbered list, max 150 words.

Deliverable

10 before/after prompts

Day 26 hours

Professional Writing

  • 1 hDemo: email, CV, cover letter and report prompts. Setting tone: formal, friendly, persuasive.
  • 3 hLab: write a CV, a cover letter for a real job post, a complaint email and a meeting summary.
  • 1 hLab: translate and polish Bangla and English text. Compare 3 tone versions.
  • 1 hPeer review: swap documents and improve each other's prompts.

Sample prompt

Write a formal cover letter for the role of [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY]. My skills: [SKILLS]. Keep it under 200 words and end with a clear call to action.

Deliverable

CV + cover letter + 2 emails

Day 36 hours

Learning, Research & Fact-Checking

  • 1 hDemo: explain a topic at 3 levels, study plans, flashcards, practice quizzes.
  • 2 hLab: create a 2-week study plan and 20 quiz questions on a subject of your choice.
  • 2 hLab: research with web search. Collect claims, then verify each against 2 sources.
  • 1 hDiscussion: hallucinations, bias, and how to spot wrong answers.

Sample prompt

Explain [TOPIC] like I am 12, then like a university student. Then give 5 quiz questions with answers.

Deliverable

Study plan + verified research sheet

Day 46 hours

Files, Data & Images

  • 1 hDemo: uploading PDFs, Word, Excel and CSV files. Asking precise questions about them.
  • 3 hLab: summarize a 20-page PDF, extract a table, and analyse a sales CSV with charts.
  • 1 hLab: upload an image or screenshot and ask for a description, text extraction or critique.
  • 1 hReview and practical quiz.

Sample prompt

I uploaded a sales CSV. Show total sales by month, the top 5 products, and one chart. Then list 3 insights in plain language.

Deliverable

Data analysis report (1 page)

Day 56 hours

Custom Instructions, Projects & Custom GPT Design

  • 1 hDemo: custom instructions, memory, and organising work into Projects.
  • 1 hDemo: what a Custom GPT is. Write its name, goal, rules, tone and knowledge files.
  • 3 hLab: write the instruction document for your own Custom GPT (for example a customer reply bot or a study tutor).
  • 1 hPeer review of instruction documents.

Sample prompt

You are [GPT NAME]. Your goal is [GOAL]. Always answer in [LANGUAGE] and [TONE]. Never [RULES]. If you do not know, say so.

Deliverable

Custom GPT instruction document

Day 66 hours

Build, Test & Business Use Cases

  • 2 hLab: build the Custom GPT, upload knowledge files, and set conversation starters.
  • 2 hLab: test with 10 real questions, log failures, and improve the instructions.
  • 2 hLab: use ChatGPT for social media captions, customer replies, lesson plans and a freelancing proposal.

Sample prompt

Create 7 Facebook posts for a [BUSINESS TYPE] in [CITY]. Include a hook, a short body, and a hashtag line.

Deliverable

Working Custom GPT + 10-test log

Day 74 hours

Assessment & Showcase

  • 1 hWritten and practical exam: 20 questions plus 3 live prompt tasks.
  • 2 hStudent presentations: demo the Custom GPT and the 5-page AI-assisted report.
  • 1 hFeedback, grading and portfolio upload.

Sample prompt

No new prompt. Students bring their own best prompts from the week.

Deliverable

Final grade: 40% report, 30% Custom GPT, 30% exam

Menu names and features in ChatGPT change often, so check the interface the day before each class.
