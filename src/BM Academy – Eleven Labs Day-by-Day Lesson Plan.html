Day-by-day lesson plan for Module 11: AI voice, script writing for audio, production, voice-over projects and voice ethics.

Day 16 hours

Text to Speech & Voice Settings

  • 1 hAccount setup, interface tour, plan limits and credits. Rule: use only scripts and voices you have the right to use.
  • 1 hDemo: voice library, voice settings (stability, similarity, style), punctuation for natural pacing, pronunciation fixes.
  • 3 hLab: narrate the same 100-word script with 5 voices and 3 setting combinations. Score each for clarity and emotion.
  • 1 hLab: test English and Bangla. Check language support and quality before using any voice for client work.

Sample task

Script preparation: short sentences, commas for pauses, numbers written as words, brand names spelled phonetically if mispronounced.

Deliverable

Voice comparison sheet (5 voices, 3 settings)

Day 26 hours

Scripts, Dialogue & Audio Production

  • 1 hDemo: writing for the ear. Short sentences, one idea per line, and clear stress points.
  • 3 hLab: produce a 2-minute narration, a two-voice dialogue and a 30-second radio-style ad. Fix mispronunciations.
  • 1 hLab: add background music and sound effects, balance the volume, and export as MP3 and WAV.
  • 1 hPeer review and practical quiz.

Sample task

Dialogue script: Voice A asks 3 customer questions, Voice B answers each in under 20 words. Friendly tone, no jargon.

Deliverable

Narration + dialogue + 30-second ad

Day 36 hours

Voice-Over Project & Ethics

  • 1 hDiscussion: voice cloning, consent, fraud risks, and labelling synthetic voices. Clone only your own voice or with written permission.
  • 3 hProject: add AI voice-over to the Invideo promo from Module 10, or to a new 60-second video. Match the timing to each scene.
  • 2 hLocalization lab: record the same video in a second language and review it with a native speaker.

Sample task

Voice-over script: 60 seconds, 8 scenes, one sentence per scene, timing marked on the video.

Deliverable

Voice-over video + second-language version

Day 42 hours

Assessment & Showcase

  • 1 hPractical exam: produce a 45-second voice-over from a written brief within 45 minutes.
  • 1 hAudio showcase, feedback and grading.

Sample task

Present the voice-over project and review it against the script and voice-ethics checklist.

Deliverable

Final grade: 60% voice-over project, 40% practical exam

Eleven Labs plans limit credits, voices and commercial use, so check the terms before Day 1. Language and voice support, including Bangla quality, changes often, so test it before class. Students must never clone or imitate someone's voice without their permission, and should label synthetic audio when they publish it.
